About Me

I am a BIM Specialist and Construction Engineer with expertise in multi-trade building information modeling, 2D drafting, coordination, and clash resolution. My work centers on producing accurate clash-free 3D models and 2D drawings that enable clients to make informed decisions and execute projects on site with efficiency.

With experience in Autodesk Revit, Navisworks, Forma, BIM360, Hexagon CADWorx, AutoCAD, and SketchUp, I specialize in developing 3D models and 2D drawings that integrate various trades such as architectural, structural, and MEPFS of a building construction project. This portfolio showcases my projects across the AEC sector.
